Our story

From a small idea to a national platform.

LoanEase PH was founded in 2019 by a group of financial technology professionals who saw a gap in the market: millions of Filipinos wanted to own smartphones, appliances, and vehicles, but couldn't afford to pay upfront — and traditional banks were too slow, too complex, and too expensive.

We built a platform that makes the lending process transparent, fast, and genuinely fair. No confusing fine print. No surprise charges. Just a straightforward agreement between us and you.

Today, we serve customers across all 7,641 islands of the Philippines, with partnerships spanning 200+ brands and retailers. We're SEC-registered and BSP-compliant, and we're proud of every loan we've approved.
